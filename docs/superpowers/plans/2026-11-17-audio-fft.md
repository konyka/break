**Goal:** Add an allocation-free, double-precision radix-2 real FFT and periodic Hann window for subsequent spectral audio processing.

**Architecture:** An immutable plan owns bit-reversal and twiddle tables. A real transform of size n packs even and odd samples into one complex transform of size n/2, then separates the conjugate-symmetric spectra; callers own all sample buffers.

**Tech Stack:** C11, the existing audio result and numeric types, CMake/CTest, libm.

## Global Constraints

- Keep the public API independent of a renderer or platform backend.
- Add no third-party dependency.
- Accept only power-of-two sizes from 4 through 65536.
- A plan is read-only after creation and may be shared only when each caller uses separate sample buffers.
- Follow the repository naming rules and keep the forbidden terminology out of new source, tests and documentation.

## Tests (`test_audio_fft`)

1. Creation accepts only powers of two in range and does not write `*out` on failure; null destruction and size queries are safe. The sentinel is a real, correctly aligned plan.
2. An impulse has a flat spectrum and a single-tone input has the known bins.
3. Every size from 4 through 1024 agrees with a direct DFT at every bin; sizes 2048 through 65536 agree at DC, Nyquist, n/4 and representative interior bins.
4. Hand-built spectra at sizes 4 and 16 agree with an independent inverse DFT; inverse transforms also restore signals and forward transforms preserve energy.
5. The transform is linear and a circular shift changes phase without changing magnitude.
6. Periodic Hann windows shifted by half their size sum to one.
7. All numerical comparisons reject NaN.
